Data + wiring notes (for the build, after sign-off)

Storage: one enum on the profile — discoverySource: app_store | instagram | facebook | tiktok | youtube | friends | other — plus discoveryAskState: answered | dismissed in AsyncStorage so the card never re-renders once handled (server field wins if they reinstall). Analytics: discovery_source_reported {source} → AppsFlyer + backend; the self-report matters most exactly where ATT blinds paid attribution. Compat: new optional profile field = additive; strict-schema rule says ship the backend field before (or with) the client (same gating discipline as the tz param). Order randomization: shuffle the six sources per user to kill position bias; "Somewhere else" always last.
