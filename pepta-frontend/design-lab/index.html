Pep’s state mirrors your level curve (NEW)
Peak · day 1–2
“You’re at 2.1 mg. This is the strong part.”
Bright, upright, quick bob.
Trough · day 6–7
“Down to 0.9 mg. Shot day’s close.”
Lower lids, slower bob. Teaches the curve without a chart.
Rest week
“Resting with you. Back on Jul 13.”
Asleep. Makes the off-cycle feel intentional, not like the app went quiet.
Milestone
“First shot logged. That’s the hard one.”
First shot · first month · first 10 lb. A reaction, not points.
Drives off medicationLevels[0].curve and cycleWindows — both already computed. Nobody else can copy this: it needs the level model. Never a sad or disappointed state for a missed dose.
